Second Trimester Care (Weeks 14 - 27)

The second trimester is often called the golden phase of pregnancy as morning sickness eases. Your baby's organs are developing, and you will begin to feel fluttering movements (quickening).

✓
Congenital Anomaly Scan (CAS): Recommended between Week 18 and 22 to assess detailed anatomy and development.
✓
Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT): Screen for gestational diabetes between Week 24 and 28.
✓
Dental Health Check: Pregnancy hormones increase risk of gum inflammation; maintain good oral hygiene.

Hospital & Birthing Bag Checklist

Prepare these essentials by Week 34 in anticipation of your delivery at the lying-in clinic:

For Mom:

✓ Valid IDs, PhilHealth/Maternity records
✓ Comfortable nursing gowns / loose shirts
✓ Maternity pads & disposable underwear
✓ Toiletries, lip balm, non-slip slippers

For Baby:

✓ 3-4 Sets of tie-side baby clothes (lampin/baru-baruan)
✓ Newborn diapers, wet wipes, cotton balls
✓ Soft receiving blankets & swaddles
✓ Mittens, booties, and baby bonnet

Labor & Delivery Signs

Head to the lying-in facility or call your midwife when contractions become regular (every 5 minutes lasting 1 minute) or if your water breaks.
